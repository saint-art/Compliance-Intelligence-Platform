Compliance Intelligence Platform

Mission

Build an enterprise-grade compliance platform that aggregates Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) from multiple trusted public sources, performs automated data mining, normalization, deduplication, confidence scoring, and exposes the data through a searchable web application and REST API.

Target Users

• Banks
• Fintech Companies
• AML Teams
• Insurance Companies
• Auditors
• Government Agencies

Primary Goal

Provide fast, accurate and explainable compliance screening for Politically Exposed Persons.
